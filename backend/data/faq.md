# ShopEase Help Centre — Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days in metro cities and 5–8 business days elsewhere in India. Express delivery (1–2 business days) is available in 40+ cities for ₹99. You will receive an SMS and email with the tracking link as soon as your order ships.

## How much does shipping cost?
Shipping is free on orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 have a ₹49 delivery charge. Express delivery costs ₹99 regardless of order value.

## How can I track my order?
Go to My Orders and select the order to see live tracking, or ask the support assistant with your order number (for example ORD-10023). Tracking updates appear within 24 hours of shipping.

## What is your return policy?
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused, in original packaging, with tags attached. Damaged, defective or wrong items can always be returned within 30 days. Innerwear, personal care products, perishables and gift cards cannot be returned.

## How do I return an item?
Ask the support assistant or go to My Orders, choose the item and select Return. We schedule a free pickup within 2 business days. Keep the item packed with its invoice and accessories.

## When will I get my refund?
Refunds are initiated after the returned item passes quality inspection, usually within 2 days of pickup. Money reaches UPI and wallets in 1–3 business days, and cards and net banking in 5–7 business days. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your bank account or as ShopEase credit.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Yes. Size and colour exchanges are free for clothing and footwear within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock. For other categories, return the item and place a new order.

## Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel any order free of charge before it ships. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery or return it after delivery.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, ShopEase wallet, EMI on cards above ₹3,000, and cash on delivery for orders up to ₹10,000.

## My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
Failed payments are automatically reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days. If the amount has not come back after 7 days, contact support with the transaction ID and we will raise it with the payment gateway.

## Do products come with a warranty?
Electronics and appliances carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year, as stated on the product page. Keep your invoice from My Orders; it is your warranty proof. ShopEase also offers a 10-day replacement for defective electronics.

## How do I change my delivery address?
You can change the address from My Orders until the order ships. After shipping, contact support and we will try to redirect the parcel with the courier.

## How do I reset my password or update my phone number?
Use "Forgot password" on the login page to reset your password with an OTP. To change your registered phone number, go to Account Settings and verify the new number with an OTP.

## Do you deliver outside India?
No, ShopEase currently ships only within India to over 19,000 PIN codes.

## How do I contact a human agent?
Ask the assistant to connect you to a person at any time. Our human support team works 9 AM to 9 PM, every day, and urgent issues are picked up within 15 minutes.

## Are my payment details safe?
Yes. Payments are processed by PCI-DSS certified gateways and ShopEase never stores your full card number or CVV.
